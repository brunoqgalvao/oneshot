Talk anywhere. It types what you meant.

Works in every app

Slack, Gmail, Cursor, Notion, your terminal. Hold fn, talk, let go.

✂︎

Cleans up as you talk

“Um”, “like” and “at 2, actually 3” come out as what you meant. Lists come out as lists.

✦

Rewrite by voice

Select text, hold fn ⌃ and say “make this friendlier” or “translate to English”.

◎

Knows where you're typing

Casual in chat, full sentences in email, exact in code and AI prompts.

⇄

Free, or your own key

30 minutes a day on us. Paste your OpenAI key for unlimited, or run it fully on-device.

↩︎

Always on the clipboard

Every dictation stays on your clipboard, and your history is one click away.

Your feedback ships.

There's a feedback box inside the app. Anything you write goes to an AI engineer (Claude Opus) running in a loop on this codebase. It fixes what it can, ships a new version, and replies to you in the app.

  1. 1You write “the dot covers my WhatsApp box”.
  2. 2Opus reads it, changes the code, runs the tests.
  3. 3A new version ships. Oneshot updates itself.
  4. 4You get a notification: “Shipped in 0.2.1”.

Why this exists

I built a version of this by hand three years ago. Then I watched Wispr Flow take off. Distribution is the whole game, and they nailed it.

Now, every time a new model comes out, I ask it to one-shot this app. This time it built the Mac app, the server, the login, the onboarding and this site, and I told it to ship. The open-source versions I tried weren't good. This one is.

It's free for anyone. If it gets too expensive to run, I'll add a paid plan. It could run fully local, but these models are so cheap now that it doesn't have to.

— Bruno Galvão
